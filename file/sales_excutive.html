JOB TITLE:

SALES EXECUTER

Job Description:

A Sales Executive is responsible for driving sales and achieving revenue targets. They engage with potential clients, build strong relationships, and provide solutions that meet customer needs. This role involves identifying new sales opportunities, negotiating deals, and collaborating with other departments to ensure customer satisfaction.

    Required skills:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, or a related field.

  • 2-3 years of experience in sales or a related field.

  • Proven track record of achieving sales targets.

      • Preferred skills:

  • Proficiency in CRM software and sales tracking tools.

  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.

  • Strong understanding of sales techniques and strategies.

      • Work Environment:

  • Office-based with frequent travel to meet clients.

  • Collaborative team environment. Regular interaction with other departments

      • Salary range:

  • The salary range for a Sales Executive typically falls between $50,000 and $70,000 annually, depending on the company's size, industry, and location.
