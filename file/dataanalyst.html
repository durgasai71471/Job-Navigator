JOB TITLE:

Data Analyst

Job Description:

A Data Analyst is responsible for collecting, processing, and analyzing data to help organizations make informed business decisions. This role involves working with various data sources, creating reports, and providing actionable insights.

    Required skills:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.

  • 2-3 years of experience in data analysis or a related field.

  • Proficiency in data analysis tools such as Excel, SQL, and Python.

      • Preferred skills:

  • Experience with data visualization tools like Tableau or Power BI.

  • Knowledge of statistical analysis and predictive modeling.

  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.

      • Work Environment:

  • Office-based with potential for remote work options.

  • Collaborative team environment with regular communication across departments.

      • Salary range:

  • The salary range for a Data Analyst typically falls between $60,000 and $90,000 annually, depending on experience, location, and company size.
