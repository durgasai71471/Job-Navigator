JOB TITLE:

ASSOCIATE ANALYST

Job Description:

The Associate Analyst role is an entry-level position designed for individuals with a keen interest in data and analytics. This role involves supporting the analytics team by conducting detailed research and analysis to provide insights that help drive business decisions. The position offers an excellent opportunity for growth and learning in a dynamic and fast-paced environment.

    Required skills:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Statistics, Data Science, or a related field.

  • Internship experience in a related field may also be considered.
