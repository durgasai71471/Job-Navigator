JOB TITLE:

MARKETING EXECUTER

Job Description:

A Marketing Executive is responsible for planning, developing, and executing marketing strategies to promote a company's products or services. This role involves working closely with the marketing team, conducting market research, creating marketing campaigns, managing social media accounts,analyzing performance data, and coordinating with other departments to ensure cohesive marketing efforts.

    Required skills:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business Administration, or a related field.

  • 2-3 years of experience in marketing or a related field.

  • Proficiency in marketing software and tools, such as Google Analytics, HubSpot, and Adobe Creative Suite.

      • Preferred skills:

  • Knowledge of SEO and SEM best practices.

  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.

  • Experience with content management systems (CMS) and customer relationship management (CRM) software.

      • Work Environment:

  • Regular interaction with other departments, such as sales, product development, and customer service.

      • Salary range:

  • The salary range for a Marketing Executive typically falls between $45,000 and $65,000 annually, depending on the company's size, industry, and location.
