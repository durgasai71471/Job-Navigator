Work_Schedule:

Testers usually work standard office hours but may need to work extra hours to meet deadlines, especially before product launches or major updates.

Growth:

Junior Tester: Focuses on executing test cases and reporting bugs.
Senior Tester: Leads testing projects and mentors junior testers.
Test Lead: Manages test plans, strategies, and teams.
QA Manager: Oversees quality assurance processes and coordinates with development teams.

JOB TITLE:

Tester

Job Description:

A Tester is responsible for evaluating and ensuring the quality of software products through manual and automated testing. This role involves identifying bugs, ensuring functionality, and collaborating with developers to improve software performance.

    Required skills:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.

  • 2-3 years of experience in software testing or a related field.

  • Proficiency in testing tools such as Selenium, JIRA, or TestRail.

      • Preferred skills:

  • Experience with automated testing frameworks.

  • Knowledge of software development lifecycle and Agile methodologies.

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

      • Work Environment:

  • Office-based with potential for remote work options.

  • Collaborative team environment with regular communication across departments.

      • Salary range:

  • The salary range for a Tester typically falls between $50,000 and $80,000 annually, depending on experience, location, and company size.
