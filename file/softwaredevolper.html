A Software Developer is responsible for designing, coding, testing, and maintaining software applications that meet user needs and business requirements. This role involves collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration and optimal performance of software solutions.

Growth:

Junior Software Developer: Focuses on writing code, debugging, and working under the guidance of senior developers.
Senior Software Developer: Leads development projects, mentors junior developers, and collaborates on design and architecture.
Software Development Manager: Oversees development teams, manages project timelines, and ensures quality standards.
Software Architect: Designs complex systems, evaluates new technologies, and defines technical standards and guidelines.

JOB TITLE:

Software Developer

Job Description:

A Software Developer is responsible for designing, coding, testing, and maintaining software applications that meet user needs and business requirements. This role involves collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration and optimal performance of software solutions.

    Required skills:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.

  • 2-3 years of experience in software development or a related field.

  • Proficiency in programming languages such as Java, Python, or C#.

      • Preferred skills:

  • Experience with software development frameworks and tools like Agile, Scrum, or DevOps.

  • Familiarity with databases and data management systems.

  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.

      • Work Environment:

  • Office-based with potential for remote work options.

  • Collaborative team environment with regular communication across departments.

      • Salary range:

  • The salary range for a Software Developer typically falls between $80,000 and $120,000 annually, depending on experience, location, and company size.
