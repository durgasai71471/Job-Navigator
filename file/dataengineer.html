Data Architect: Develops the overall data strategy and designs scalable solutions.

JOB TITLE:

Data Engineer

Job Description:

A Data Engineer is responsible for building and maintaining the architecture that enables data generation, collection, and usage. This role involves developing data pipelines, optimizing data systems, and supporting data scientists and analysts in obtaining actionable insights.

    Required skills:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.

  • 3-5 years of experience in data engineering or software development.

  • Strong programming skills in languages such as Python, Java, or Scala.

  • Experience with ETL tools and big data technologies like Hadoop or Spark.

      • Preferred skills:

  • Experience with cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.

  • Familiarity with data warehousing solutions such as Redshift or Snowflake.

  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.

      • Work Environment:

  • Office-based with potential for remote work options.

  • Collaborative team environment with regular communication across departments.

      • Salary range:

  • The salary range for a Data Engineer typically falls between $80,000 and $120,000 annually, depending on experience, location, and company size.
