Work_Schedule:

Front-End Developers typically work full-time, standard office hours. However, they may need to work overtime to meet project deadlines or resolve urgent issues.

Growth:

Junior Front-End Developer: Focuses on building and maintaining user interfaces under supervision.
Senior Front-End Developer: Leads front-end projects, mentors junior developers, and ensures design consistency.
Front-End Lead: Manages front-end teams and coordinates with other departments to deliver high-quality products.
Front-End Architect: Designs complex front-end architectures and oversees the implementation of new technologies and frameworks.

JOB TITLE:

Front-End Developer

Job Description:

A Front-End Developer is responsible for implementing visual elements that users interact with in a web application. This role requires close collaboration with back-end developers and designers to create functional and aesthetically pleasing interfaces.

    Required skills:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Web Development, or a related field.

  • 2-3 years of experience in front-end development or a related field.

  • Proficiency in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks like React or Angular.

      • Preferred skills:

  • Experience with responsive design and mobile-first development.

  • Familiarity with version control systems like Git.

  • Strong understanding of UI/UX principles.

      • Work Environment:

  • Office-based with potential for remote work options.

  • Collaborative team environment with regular communication across departments.

      • Salary range:

  • The salary range for a Front-End Developer typically falls between $70,000 and $110,000 annually, depending on experience, location, and company size.
